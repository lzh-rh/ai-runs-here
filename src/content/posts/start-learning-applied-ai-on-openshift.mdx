This is a reading guide, not a product lab. It explains how to use this personal publication and how its notes separate completed work from work still in progress.

## Start with the evidence state

A field note should say whether its result was observed or is still pending. An experiment-in-progress label means the note is not evidence that a product workflow succeeded.

## Check the environment details

When a note reports a lab result, its environment section should name the exact product and platform versions used. A product lab without those details should remain a draft and stay outside the production site.

## Choose a topic

Choose the topic that matches the question you are exploring: [OpenShift Lightspeed](/topics/openshift-lightspeed/), [Agentic Lightspeed](/topics/agentic-lightspeed/), or [MCP](/topics/mcp/).

## Keep the source of record close

AI Runs Here is a personal learning publication. For product behavior and support details, follow the official documentation linked from a field note.
